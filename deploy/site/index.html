The failure boundary

BEFORE AUDIO

Retry with a reset.

A real primary-process kill is followed by a completed turn through the secondary. Token previews are explicitly reset before retry.

AFTER AUDIO

Fail without repetition.

Once speech is visible, retry could repeat it. The gateway ends that turn instead of silently replaying audible content.

ON INTERRUPTION

Fence the old generation.

A gateway mutex guards generation checks and writes. The browser stops scheduled audio; old worker frames cannot cross the cancellation acknowledgement.

02 / FOLLOW THE TURN

Speech, tokens, audio. Then interrupt.

The actual authenticated console processes public speech, shows stage timings, stops playback, completes a replacement turn, and reloads persisted timing history.

03 / THE SYSTEM, WITH ITS LIMITS

Go gateway→Energy VAD→Whisper→SmolLM2→Piper

Bounded native work

Cancellation drops output and cancels transport. An ongoing native operation can still finish; its admission slot remains occupied until it returns.

Durable timing history

A flushed, single-process journal records IDs, outcomes and stage-boundary timings. It retains no prompt, transcript or audio.

Actual CPU inference

Transformers and open speech models run locally. vLLM/GPU throughput, continuous microphone capture and container restore remain unfinished.

Partial, explicitly.

The interactive gateway and model services are not hosted on this site. The local demonstration uses complete utterances and sentence audio chunks. Small-model language quality, capture-to-ear latency, replicated history and broad voice-domain robustness are not established.
